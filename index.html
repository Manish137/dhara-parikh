About

About Dhara Parikh

Balancing creative vision with business objectives — from first sketch to the shop floor.

Dhara Parikh is a graphic and brand designer with more than 15 years of experience. As Brand Manager at Spacewood she helped take a manufacturer into retail; as an art director and studio designer she worked on identity, campaigns and collateral; and as a freelancer she has delivered brand identity, advertising and packaging for clients across India, Denmark and the USA. She has also led UX and media work, and brings that thinking to digital touchpoints.

Trained first as an architect (B.Arch, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology), with graduate credits toward an MFA in graphic design at the Academy of Art University, San Francisco, she brings a spatial, typographic sensibility to retail installations, books and packaging. She has also designed curriculum and taught as a volunteer design educator, and today works from Tracy, California.
