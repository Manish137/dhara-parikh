About Dhara Parikh

Balancing creative vision with business objectives — from first sketch to the shop floor.

Dhara Parikh is a graphic designer, brand strategist and UI/UX designer with more than 15 years of experience. She has led cross-functional teams, developed scalable branding systems and delivered marketing campaigns for clients across India, Denmark and the USA.

Trained first as an architect (B.Arch, Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology) and then in graphic design at the Academy of Art University, San Francisco, she brings a spatial, typographic sensibility to everything from retail installations and coffee table books to packaging and digital interfaces. She has also designed curriculum and taught as a volunteer design educator, and today works from Tracy, California.
