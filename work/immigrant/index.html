Immigrant

Experimental retail · performance, posters, merchandise

The immigrant student of India, who returns with a transformed personality, is usually in conflict with a deep-rooted identity. However, the best of all worlds, with experience and an eye for acceptance, can be an additive to this identification and personality.

The Immigrant NRI council experimented with witty visuals and merchandise to promote a sense of connection to the global one world that we imbibe from.
