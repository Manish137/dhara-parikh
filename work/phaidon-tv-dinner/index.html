Phaidon TV Dinner

Typeface creation · poster and brochure

As designers, we are often expected to take vastly different ideas to come up with a creative solution with originality, wit and humour.

The aim of this kitchen project was to select a brand and a product that is entirely unrelated to that brand, to create a refreshing brand image through the promotion of the chosen product. This amounted to the creation of the Phaidon TV Dinner typeface.
