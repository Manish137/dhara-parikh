The Faze

A pictorial narrative · book design

The Faze is a collective celebration of ideas, images, innovation and the restlessness of the creative flux within the inside and the outside world. Layered photographs and esoteric colours frame the project, and the flow of typography with its partial hide-and-show effect further justifies the theme of the book.

The images, objects and script that fall into various categories have been shaped and modelled together by this interweaving experience of within and without oneself.
