Lego

Lego range of typography · product imagined

Lego introduces ‘League Of Its Own’, a range of typography sets. It brings a game that is built by the player — customised and different every time. It is an opportunity to hone the creative instincts of all ages with a playful and learning incentive.

Lego serves the typography set to encourage the builders and creators of a unique product. The colours used are the usual Lego brick colours, made more subtle to suit the sophistication of the product; the introduction of the sheep makes it more identifiable with the company and its clientele over the years.
