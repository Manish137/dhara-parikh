Garage Sale

Transformation of vernacular communication

America has a long-going popular tradition of organising garage sales. However, the concept is refined and polished by this organisation that upscales the sale. All the unwanted items are turned into big profits via streamlined advertising and planning of the overall process.

The drop of ‘b’ in your ‘garbage’ sophisticates the entire concept of ‘garage’ sale. The merchandise ranges from info products like posters and brochures, to commercial products like shopping bags and carry-aways.

Client
U.S. garage sale organisation
Genre

Role
