Nordstrom

Designer identity collection · advertising campaign

Client as credited in the original portfolio; commission status not confirmed

Nordstrom has always excelled in offering fashion collections customised to every consumer. This season, the idea revolved around bringing the many facets of a woman to the fore.

An interesting layering of sketched emotions with the thought-provoking lyrics of the Pet Shop Boys has been experimented with to capture the mood of the collection. The play of scale in typography and colour makes it cleverly sharp yet subtle.
