Identity & Multiculturalism

Transforming map · navigational book

Creative interpretation project

The project consists of two parts that convey the idea that identity is not static but constantly transforming, and also celebrate the journey of an identity that grows out of the strength of diverse cultures.

The visual narrative of the Map expresses the transitional moment where diverse cultures meet to transform into a stronger, more flexible identity that can respond to unpredictability, change and differences. The Book functions as a navigational guide that provides context to the Map.
