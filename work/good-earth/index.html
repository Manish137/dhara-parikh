Good Earth

goodearthindia.org · website, price tags and evites

Good Earth is based on values of sustainability. Encouraging quality craftsmanship and the revival of lost Indian traditions beneficial to the environment and society is its core value.

The project was to revamp the website and create customised price tags to justify the art style of handloom. It was further extended to evites and event organisation.
