Everything In Between

Coffee table book · typographic experimentation

Client as credited in the original portfolio; commission status not confirmed

The project consists of a journey, which spans everything in between — from nothing being safe to understanding that change is the only constant.

This journey is narrated through words, concepts and strokes of ink through typographic experimentation: a coffee table experiential book to simply stir different emotions and a story varying each time it is held.
