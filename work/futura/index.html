Futura

Typeface promotion kit

Futura (created in Germany, 1928) formed the core of Bauhaus ideology — strict geometric forms and a lack of any embellishment. Based on the same ideology, this slipcase contains an elegant display and an intensive study of the typeface, followed by a thorough exploration of not only its traditional but also its unconventional handling.

The Wolfgang Weingart typographic process was used, characterised by his painterly application of graphical and typographical elements: the typographic drafts are thus compelling yet lucid, free yet controlled.
