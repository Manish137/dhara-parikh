Medical Analog Tape

The reinvention of time and its parameters

The project involved redefining time in parameters suitable for a cardiac patient. The format is that of the traditionally used medical analog tape that records the patient’s heart rate per second. The measurement is of anxiety levels with respect to the varying heart rate, based on the consciousness and activity during long surgery hours.

The project was a definition of a subjective experience in iconography and typography that is medically analytical and true to its kind. Suitable packaging formed an extension to the project.
