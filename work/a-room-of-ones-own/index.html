A Room of One’s Own

Type play with context and meaning

The designer’s role is to interpret context to effectively communicate to the audience. My intention was to explore context and meaning through the spatial and typographic articulation of Virginia Woolf’s ‘A Room Of One’s Own’.

The aim of the project was to engage the audience with the content through space and type. The physical space and typography were to propel the audience to experience a room of their own. The application was through vinyl letterings on a window, with a differing viewing experience depending on the day and weather.
