Silence

A self-exploration narrative

Silence is a product of experimentation and self-exploration, expressing the emotion of being unheard, voiceless and anonymous. The times are metaphorical, categorical and also directed to the extant circumstances in different parts of the world. The word in itself inherently carries an endless admix of emotions suppressed in the beat of circumstances.

The project involved maintaining a visual journal to gain the essence of a typical life. The illustrations, pictures and quotes are a direct pick from this journal, compiled together sequentially as a coffee table book.
