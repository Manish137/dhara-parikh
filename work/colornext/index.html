ColorNext

Experimental retail installation

A collaborative engagement between leaders of the Indian design fraternity and Asian Paints’ experienced color marketing team, Color Next combines the science of research and documentation with visionary design sensibility and creative intuition.

The result is an expertly curated, trendsetting series of color stories that unfold an inspired design vocabulary for the next year. The work spans retail posters, merchandise and stationery.

Client
Asian Paints
Genre
Experimental retail
Role
Concept, art direction, installation
Specs
Retail posters, 1900 mm × 600 mm (set of 6)
